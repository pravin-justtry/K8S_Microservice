1. Check the release:
   kubectl get pods,svc -n {{ .Release.Namespace }}

2. Frontend NodePort:
   kubectl get svc frontend -n {{ .Release.Namespace }}

3. Frontend LoadBalancer:
   kubectl get svc frontend-external -n {{ .Release.Namespace }}

Internal service DNS names are the Kubernetes Service names, for example:
   cartservice:7070
   productcatalogservice:3550
   checkoutservice:5050
